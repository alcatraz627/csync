## Rulings I would change

- B13 drops T-06 without listing it as a conflict. T-06 asks Widgets to "mark xkcd Built and media remote, Quick Settings, app shortcuts Planned". The mock now shows Media remote, the Quick Settings tiles and app shortcuts with an "Add" action, as if they exist. If native has not built them, the app will claim capability it does not have, which is the dishonesty T-06 was about. Either show only what exists in native, or bring T-06 to the owner.
- A4 keeps Notes in a More section literally titled "Areas". MO-04 complained "Pi Notes sits in Areas". The reasoning may be right, but the frame reproduces the exact words of the complaint. Rename the section, and show the owner this one since it overrides their call-out.
- Three product choices are in the mock with no row in the decisions doc: Pi playback starts muted (finding 38), the Pi online dot moved from the rows to the heading (CH-22, finding 37), and Loop has three states against P-07's toggle (finding 23). Each needs a row.
