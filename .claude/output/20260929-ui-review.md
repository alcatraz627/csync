# csync redesign: fresh-eyes visual review

**Date:** 2026-09-29 · reviewer: fresh-eyes designer (did not build this)
**Screens reviewed:** Home, More, Tools, Media, Chat, Settings (native emulator captures)
**Reference:** the approved Home mock (`home_new.png`) and the diagnostic at
`/Users/alcatraz627/Code/Claude/csync/.claude/output/20260929-ui-diagnostic.md`.

## Verdict

The redesign lands the two hardest goals on Home. Home now has a real hero (big
warm title, the mesh graphic that finally echoes the app icon, coral eyebrow,
green live state, capability pills), so hierarchy and identity are genuinely
present where the old build had a flat device row. The placeholder-underscore
tell (`Volume _`) is gone, and coral filled primary buttons (Recheck, New chat)
give each screen one clear primary action.

But the direction is only half-rolled across the app. Two of the five screens
still wear the exact generic tells the diagnostic said to kill, the mock's best
element was downgraded on the way into native, and coral has been spread onto
everything again so it no longer signals anything. This reads as a strong Home
bolted to three screens that got a lighter pass and two that barely changed. It
is not yet one designed product.

## Findings, ranked by visual impact

### 1. Section headers are inconsistent across screens, and two still use the ALL-CAPS tell (highest impact)
`More`, `Tools`, and `Media` use sentence or title case section headers (`Areas`,
`Service health`, `Choose a drive`). Good, that is the fix. But `Settings` still
uses ALL-CAPS tracked eyebrows (`DEVICES & CONNECTIONS`, `MEDIA & DISPLAY`,
`ASSISTANT`, `APP`) and `Chat` still uses `RECENT CONVERSATIONS`. These are the
number-one generic signature the diagnostic named, surviving intact on two
screens. Worse, because three sibling screens fixed it, swiping between tabs
shows the inconsistency directly: the ALL-CAPS screens read as the old template
sitting next to the new ones. **Fix:** convert every section header on Settings
and Chat to the same sentence-case treatment the other three screens already
use, so casing is one rule app-wide.

### 2. Home's resume card was downgraded from the mock's richest element to a plain row
In the approved mock, "Pick up where you left off" is the standout beat: a media
card with a thumbnail, a coral play button, and a progress bar reading
"6:29 left" over "F-Droid 2.0 ... YouTube on Pi screen". In native it is now a
generic list row, `UI-control-check / Last conversation` with a chevron, visually
identical to a settings link. The progress bar, the coral play affordance, and
the sense that media is resumable are all gone. This was the mock's clearest
proof that csync is about reaching and playing on the Pi, and native drops it.
**Fix:** rebuild the resume card to the mock: leading thumbnail or media glyph,
title plus source line, a coral play button on the right, and the progress bar.
If there is genuinely no media to resume, show a real empty state, not a chat row
dressed as a resume card.

### 3. Coral is overloaded again, and it collides with warning state
The diagnostic asked for coral on "the one primary action and live state,
nothing else." Instead every icon tile on every list screen is coral (Settings,
More, Tools, Media all show full columns of coral squares), plus section-header
icons, active tabs, and the primary buttons. When everything is coral, the
primary button no longer stands out. The sharpest case is Tools: the
"Undervoltage now" warning triangle is the same coral as the healthy
"Pi media service" icon and the "Recheck" button, so a real fault reads as
routine. Live state already uses green correctly, which proves the palette can
carry meaning. **Fix:** demote the routine icon tiles to a neutral or dim
treatment and reserve coral for the primary action and truly active state; give
warnings and faults a distinct warning hue (amber or red) so undervoltage does
not look like a normal row.

### 4. Middle-dot meta strings survive nearly everywhere
The diagnostic called these out as a tell to replace with pills or plain
sentences. They are still on almost every screen: `1 thread · assistant ready`
and `Mac offline · choose a device` on Home, `Pi online · saved threads` and
`1h ago · 3 messages` on Chat, `Disconnected · connect this drive` on Media,
`Undervoltage now · Use a suitable Pi power supply` and `Planned · capture a
trace during lag` on Tools. **Fix:** turn these into plain sentences or split
the two facts into a title line and a quieter status line, the way the hero card
already does with its green dot plus "Reachable now over Tailscale".

### 5. List screens read sparse and half-finished (low density returns)
Media shows a search field, four tabs, two disconnected drive rows, then roughly
sixty percent of the screen is dead black. Chat has the same void below the New
chat button. This is the "sparse, half-finished" feeling the diagnostic flagged,
coming back on the list screens. Media is the worst because both drives are
disconnected and there is no empty-state treatment at all, just a large black
gap. **Fix:** give Media a real disconnected empty state (an illustration or a
reassuring "connect a drive to browse" panel that occupies the space), and let
Chat's void carry a lightweight prompt or recent-activity hint rather than
nothing.

### 6. Settings card grouping is inconsistent with itself
Under `DEVICES & CONNECTIONS` three rows share one card with dividers, but
`MEDIA & DISPLAY`, `ASSISTANT`, and `APP` each get a full ALL-CAPS header over a
single standalone card. The result is a long stack of near-identical single-row
cards where the only differentiator is the label text, the exact pattern the
diagnostic named. **Fix:** group related single settings under fewer headers, or
drop the one-row-per-section headers and let a single grouped card carry the
rows, so the screen is not one header per line.

### 7. Home hero dropped the fourth capability pill
The mock's hero shows four pills across two rows: Media, Assistant, Camera,
**Screen**. Native shows only three (Media, Assistant, Camera) and the "Screen"
pill is missing, though there is room for it. Minor, but it is a silent
divergence from the approved mock and it drops the one capability (screen and
playback) that is central to the product story. **Fix:** restore the Screen pill.

### 8. Uneven card heights from wrapping subtitles (minor)
On Home the Share card subtitle wraps to two lines while its row-mate Camera is
one line, so the two cards in that grid row are different heights. Same on Tools
between Pi power (two lines) and App performance (one line). **Fix:** set a
minimum card height per grid row or cap the subtitle to one line with a
consistent truncation.

## What is genuinely good (briefly)
- Home hero is a real hero and the mesh graphic finally ties the UI to the app
  icon. This is the strongest single improvement.
- Breadcrumbs (`Home / More`, `Home / Settings`) add wayfinding and a bit of
  identity across sub-screens.
- Green-for-live and grey-for-offline dots read correctly and prove the palette
  can carry state.
- The placeholder-underscore tell is gone; volume now routes to a real
  "Pi playback volume" row.

## Capture note (not a design defect)
The primary resume item and the only conversation both read `UI-control-check`,
which looks like debug data. If these are shipping or approval screenshots,
seed one or two realistic conversation and media items first so the hero-adjacent
content does not read as a test string.
