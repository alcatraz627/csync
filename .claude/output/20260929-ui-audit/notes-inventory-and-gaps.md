## Part 2. Gaps and inconsistencies

Ordered by how likely each is to mislead someone.

### Contradictions between documents

1. **Accent set: three different answers.** `/Users/alcatraz627/Code/Claude/csync/docs/android-ui-system.md:85-96` lists eight accents including Gold and calls Blue, Leaf, Rose, Gold "proposed". `docs/android-ui-round4-reconciliation.md:13` says "eight accent samples". Owner rows SE-05 to SE-08 ("Remove the gold color", replace with a persistent custom picker) and `docs/android-ui-round5-plan.md:35` and `docs/android-ui-final-spec.md:32` say seven colors plus Custom, no Gold. The design-system doc was never updated, and it is the one a new reader opens for color. It also still says "The four additions are not yet Android settings", which is no longer accurate (call-out `co-20260928-001940-bf` and the checkpoint describe Appearance work).

2. **Design-system doc predates every round 5 decision.** `docs/android-ui-system.md` (last edited 26 Sep 18:39) still says "The exact destination set remains open for owner review" (line 53), has no breadcrumb rule, no drawer rule (round 5: one modal drawer, no close buttons), no icon-left rule, no "no ellipsis" rule, no title+subtitle rule, no chevron rule, and defines the transport as "Pause or Resume, seek, Stop" rather than the round 5 single row (Favorite, Rewind, Pause, Forward, Stop plus skip dropdown) with a 2x2 Volume/Speed/Rotate/Loop grid. It describes Search as a "global search proposal" though S-01 makes it a required route.

3. **Root and hub READMEs and the design brief are stale.** `/Users/alcatraz627/Code/csync-hub/README.md` says Tools is "the xkcd widget companion plus a live Shizuku process monitor", "four accent colours", five surfaces Home/Share/Chat/Tools/Settings. The app now has Home, Media, Share, Chat, More (Camera, Tools, Settings, Notes, Pins, Search). `docs/ui-design-brief.md` still lists "Five surfaces: Home, Share, Chat, Tools, Settings" as ratified decision 1, which conflicts with the five-destination set in the final spec (Home, Media, Share, Chat, More). Neither the README nor the brief mentions Media, the Pi media service on 8792, Notes, Pins, Camera, or the Pi update flow. AGENTS.md is the only place that lists six tabs plus Media.

4. **Tab count.** AGENTS.md says "preserve six-tab navigation" (Home, Share, Chat, Tools, Settings, Camera per its own description), while `docs/android-ui-final-spec.md:7` says "five primary areas remain Home, Media, Share, Chat, More". Round 5 line 15 says "keep the five existing destinations". AGENTS.md was not updated, and its "Preserve ... six-tab navigation unless the owner decides otherwise" reads as a standing constraint that the shipped nav already violates.

5. **Route count.** Round 5 plan and final spec say 28 routes. `callouts.jsonl` rechecks say "29 routes" in several places (for example `co-20260927-084558-cc`, `co-20260927-084547-a5`). Not reconciled; one of them counts a redirect route.

6. **Search "B" pick versus "not a real variant".** The ledger flags this (Conflict 2) and round 4 doc says "use B", while the ledger says treat Search as a from-scratch build, not variant B. The round 4 and round 5 docs still say "B". The ledger's resolution exists only in the ledger.

7. **Home hierarchy naming is resolved only in the ledger.** Round 4 doc line 19 and 55 still say "Open an area"; the rename to Capabilities appears in round 5 (line 22) but round 4 has no supersede note. The ledger's Conflict 1 states the resolution; the docs do not cross-reference each other.

8. **Ledger versus its own status file, and status versus reality.** `ledger-status.md` (last generated 00:55 on 29 Sep) says app UNBUILT for C-02 to C-06 (cover gallery), CH-08/CH-09 (attach drawer), P-11/P-12 (pending Rotate/Loop cancel on Stop), SH-09 (Instagram), CH-18. The kit checkpoint later the same day says chat attach, Stop-cancels-pending, and the model/effort Save drawer are done. `working-order.md` "Known gaps" also still lists chat attach and model Save button as not built. The status file is behind the checkpoint by several rows, and the checkpoint says "add overrides there with evidence" but no override block exists in the status file.
